What you get

Lumen Circle is a dashboard for Apple Home and Philips Hue. The app runs on your iPhone, your iPad, your Mac and your Apple Watch and talks directly to HomeKit and, if you connect it, to your Hue Bridge on the home network. There is a single version with every feature; there is no second tier.

The first four days

After installing it, you can use Lumen Circle in full for four days, without paying and without creating an account. After that the app stays locked until a subscription is running. Your settings are kept.

Subscription, price and cancellation

  • Term: one year, then it renews automatically for a further year each time.
  • Price: CHF 8 a year. Outside Switzerland the amount is the one the App Store states in your currency; what counts is always the price shown to you before you subscribe.
  • Billing: through your Apple ID. You are charged when you subscribe and then at every renewal, within the last 24 hours before the current year ends.
  • Cancellation: at any time in your Apple ID settings under “Subscriptions”, at the latest 24 hours before the term ends. The subscription then runs to the end of the period you paid for and does not renew.
  • Devices: The subscription covers every device with the same Apple ID and can be brought to a new device with “Restore purchase”.

Your contract partner for the payment is Apple, not the provider of this app. Refunds are handled by Apple; you make the request at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What the app needs

A home in Apple’s Home app. Without accessories set up, Lumen Circle has nothing to show. The energy meter also needs a Philips Hue Bridge on the same network. Notifications, the leaving alert and Party mode each require a permission from iOS; refuse one of them and you lose exactly that feature and nothing else.

What the provider does not answer for

Lumen Circle shows and switches what HomeKit and the Hue Bridge make available. Whether your accessories are reachable, whether your sensors measure correctly and whether an automation from your manufacturer does what it should is outside this app’s influence.

The app is not a security system and not an alarm system. The leaving alert and the threshold alerts are hints, not a guarantee: they depend on iOS waking the app, on the device having power and network, and on the sensors reporting. Do not rely on Lumen Circle for safety, health or fire protection.

The energy figures are calculated, not measured. Hue lamps do not measure their consumption. Lumen Circle works it out from model, switch state, brightness and colour, with curves from the open Powercalc project. For known models that is close; for unknown ones it stays an estimate. The numbers are good for comparing and for getting a sense of scale, not as the basis for a bill.

The provider’s liability is limited to intent and gross negligence, as far as the law permits.

Your data

Lumen Circle has no servers. What the app knows about your home stays on your device. The details are in the Privacy Policy.
